# Bumpora support

For help with Bumpora, email [yuvalpesi@gmail.com](mailto:yuvalpesi@gmail.com).

Include your app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send medical documents, Apple Account passwords, health measurements, or invitation links. If sending a screenshot, remove personal and medical details first.

For privacy information and data deletion, open Together → Settings and privacy → Privacy and information management. The full privacy policy is also available inside the app without an internet connection.

Bumpora is an organization app and does not provide medical advice or emergency care. For medical questions, contact your healthcare professional; for emergencies, contact local emergency services.
